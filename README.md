## Course structure

Built alongside the **PLA — Português Língua de Acolhimento** programme at [NIALP — Associação Intercultural Lisboa](https://nialp.pt). 150 hours across 7 units; concludes with an official A2 certificate.

### Nível A1

Each UFCD runs for **25 hours** (19h synchronous + 6h asynchronous). The programme concludes with an official A2 certificate.

### UFCD 6452 — Eu e a minha rotina diária ✅ S1–8

**Level:** A1 · **Focus:** Personal identification and daily life

**By the end of this unit you should be able to:**
spell and use the alphabet; introduce yourself and others; give and request personal and professional information; fill in forms; locate in space and time; describe people and places; talk about daily actions and actions in progress; tell the time; identify transport and ask for ticket prices.

| Content area          | Key topics                                                                                                                                                              | Grammar                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Identificação pessoal | Countries, nationalities, languages, addresses, phone numbers, civil status, DOB, age, sex, qualifications, forms/visa/cards, physical description, family, house, city | Pronouns, Ser/Estar, Ter, present indicative -AR verbs, gender/plural of nouns, definite articles, prepositions em/de, interrogatives, possessives, demonstratives                         |
| Vida quotidiana       | Meals, parts of day, times, timetables, transport, daily habits, prices, money, tickets, traffic                                                                        | Estar + adjective, present indicative -ER/-IR verbs, reflexive verbs, irregular verbs (ir/vir/ver/ler/sair/dormir/haver), estar a + inf., ter de + inf., prepositions of movement and time |

## European Portuguese vs Brazilian

This reference focuses specifically on **European Portuguese**.

| Feature           | EU Portuguese            | Brazilian Portuguese |
| ----------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Unstressed vowels | Swallowed / reduced      | Open and clear       |
| Ongoing action    | estar **a** + infinitivo | gerund (-ando/-endo) |
| Train             | **comboio**              | trem                 |
| Bus               | **autocarro**            | ônibus               |
| Bus stop          | **paragem**              | parada               |
| Juice             | **sumo**                 | suco                 |
